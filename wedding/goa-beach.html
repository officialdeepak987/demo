FAQ

Questions? We've Got Answers

Everything you need to know before booking your dream beach decoration in Goa.

We recommend booking at least 3-7 days in advance, especially during peak season (November to February). For high-demand dates like Valentine's Day, New Year's Eve, or long weekends, booking 2-4 weeks ahead is advisable. That said, we also accommodate last-minute bookings (24-48 hours) subject to availability — just WhatsApp us!

We cover all major beaches in Goa including Baga, Calangute, Anjuna, Vagator, Palolem, Colva, Agonda, Arambol, Candolim, Sinquerim, Morjim, and Mandrem. We also service smaller, more secluded beaches upon request. Contact us with your preferred location and we'll confirm availability.

Sunset time (5:30 PM to 7:30 PM) is the most magical for romantic setups — the golden light makes everything glow beautifully. Night setups (7 PM onwards) with candles and fairy lights are equally stunning. For proposals or surprises, we recommend timing the reveal around sunset for the most photogenic moments.

Absolutely! Every package can be customised. You can add or remove elements, choose specific flower types and colors, request custom name/message boards, add a bonfire, live music, a personal chef, photographer, drone videography, and much more. Just WhatsApp us your wish list and we'll create a tailored package for you.

Booking is simple! WhatsApp us with your event details → we share a proposal and design → you confirm and pay a 50% advance to lock the date → balance is paid on the day of the event. We accept UPI, bank transfer, and cash. Once booked, your date and beach are secured exclusively for you.

We monitor weather forecasts closely and will inform you in advance if heavy rain is expected. In most cases, we can reschedule to the next available date at no extra charge. For light rain, we have contingency plans including tent setups and covered areas near the beach. Your event experience is our priority.

Yes! We can arrange professional photography, videography, and even drone footage as add-ons to any package. Our Ocean Romance and Royal Sunset packages include photography. For custom packages, we'll quote based on your requirements — typically 1 hour of photography starts at ₹2,500.

Yes, we are committed to eco-friendly practices. We use biodegradable materials, collect all our items after the event, and leave beaches cleaner than we found them. We hold all necessary local permissions for beach setups. No plastic, no permanent fixtures — just beautiful, responsible celebrations.
