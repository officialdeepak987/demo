The Planning & Setup Process

How we coordinate to produce flawless surprises on Andaman's sandy shores.

01. Conceptual Design

Consultation & Mood Board

We discuss your dream idea, occasion, and select colors. We compile customized design mockups and align on floral layouts and budget estimates.

02. Permits & Venue Prep

Forest Clearances & Location Locking

We take care of all forest and tourism permits. If hosted on resort beachfronts, we coordinate logistics with resort management seamlessly.

03. Handcrafted Preparation

Florals & Props Polishing

Our team handpicks fresh roses and orchids on the morning of the event. We clean structures, inspect light bulbs, and pre-assemble signs.

04. On-Site Magic Execution

Rigging & Setup Coordination

We rig the bamboo structures, drape linens, layout carpets/cushions, set candles, and light them up exactly 30 minutes before your surprise entry time.
