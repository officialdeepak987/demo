Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know
about skin care

Find clear and helpful answers to your most common skin care questions. Expert guidance on treatments, routines, and results.

Q1. How do I know which treatment is right for me?
+
The best treatment depends on your specific skin type, concerns, and goals. Our specialists conduct a comprehensive skin assessment during your first consultation to recommend the most suitable treatment plan tailored just for you.
Q2. How long do results from cosmetic treatments last?
+
The duration varies depending on the type of procedure and your skin condition. Laser treatments typically last 6–12 months, while PRP and anti-aging treatments can provide results lasting 1–2 years with proper maintenance.
Q3. Can I combine multiple treatments in one plan?
+
Absolutely! Many of our patients benefit from combining treatments. Our dermatologists will design a personalized multi-treatment plan to address multiple skin concerns simultaneously for optimal results.
Q4. What should I do before and after treatment?
+
Before treatment, avoid sun exposure and certain skincare products (we'll advise you specifically). After treatment, follow our post-care instructions, use prescribed moisturizers, and protect your skin from direct sunlight for the best healing and results.
