What We Brew

Crafted with Intention

Every method, every bean, every pour — chosen with care for the cup you deserve.

Espresso

Intense, syrupy, and caramel-edged. Pulled at exactly 9 bars for a ristretto-style extraction that coats the palate.

Signature Latte

Velvety micro-foam poured over double shots — choose oat, almond, or whole milk. Seasonal syrups on request.

Pour Over

Single-origin beans. Gooseneck kettle. Four minutes of patience. The most honest cup we know how to make.

Cold Brew

18-hour steep. Naturally sweet, never bitter. Served over hand-chipped ice with a salted caramel float.

Our Philosophy

Why Choose Us?

We believe great coffee is a conversation between the farmer, the roaster, and the barista — with you at the end of it.

Direct Trade BeansWe partner directly with farmers in Ethiopia, Colombia, and Guatemala — cutting out middlemen and ensuring fair wages.
Roasted LocallySmall-batch roasting every Tuesday. Beans on your table within 72 hours of roast — never stale, always vibrant.
Trained BaristasEach team member spends 200 hours learning the craft before pulling a single shot. Precision is our culture.
